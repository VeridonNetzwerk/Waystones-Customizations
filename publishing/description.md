# Waystones Customizations

**Your waystone list, but readable and good-looking.**

Once you have 30 waystones, the [Waystones](https://modrinth.com/mod/waystones) menu becomes a long grey list. Waystones Customizations fixes that:

### 🎨 46 Minecraft-style themes, with live preview
Oak, Cherry, Deepslate, Nether Fortress, End City, Ocean Monument, Amethyst, Honeycomb, the classic inventory look and many more, sorted into categories.
Click the button next to the menu, hover a theme to try it, click to keep it.
Backgrounds use the block textures of your game, so they match your resource pack. The theme covers every Waystones window (list, group editor, icon picker, popups) and leaves the rest of the game alone.

### 🖌️ A background for every waystone
Hover an entry and click the brush (or right click it). Pick the group colour, one of 16 colours, 70+ block textures or your own picture, for one waystone or for a whole group.
By default every entry is tinted in its group colour, so you can tell your bases, farms and dungeons apart at a glance.

### 🏰 48 hand-drawn group icons
Pixel-art icons made for this mod in the Minecraft item style: home, castle, village, farm, harbor, mine, grass block, mountains, desert, diamonds, dungeon, nether portal, end portal and more. Plus **200+ Minecraft items** as icons. No texture pack needed.

### 🔲 Icon picker with tabs
Places, Nature, Resources, Danger, Worlds, Waystones, Items and Your own. Scroll with the mouse wheel, see the name of every icon.

### 📁 Your own pictures, no texture pack
Drop a PNG into `config/waystonescustomizations/icons` or `config/waystonescustomizations/hintergruende`, click "Reload" in game, done. The folder buttons in the menus open the folders for you.

---

**Requires:** Waystones
**Side:** client only (servers don't need it). Group icons are stored on the server by Waystones, so other players need this mod to see our icons. Your own pictures are only visible to players with the same file.
**Loaders:** Fabric and NeoForge
**Versions:** 26.1.2, 26.2, 26.3
